Brunel University of London 2025–2026 Fusion 360 · Makeblock

Autonomous robot

A prototype autonomous robot built on a modular structural kit, navigating with onboard ultrasonic and line-follower sensors. I designed, documented, built, programmed and tested it.

  • Modelled the full robot as a 3D assembly in Autodesk Fusion 360: chassis from structural beams, plates and brackets, with the MegaPi controller, sensors, drive wheels and battery pack positioned before anything was built.
  • Produced a formal technical assembly drawing with ballooned item references, an itemised parts list (descriptions, quantities, materials) and a completed title block.
  • Built the physical robot from the CAD design, integrating controller, sensors, drive and power supply into one working system.
  • Programmed autonomous navigation in Makeblock, then traced each fault to design, assembly or code and fixed it through repeated test-and-refine cycles.
